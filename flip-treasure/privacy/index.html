Privacy policy

Flip Treasure (package name in.pingul.flip), published by Pingul.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who we are

Pingul makes Flip Treasure, a single-player puzzle game for Android. This page describes how the game stores data on your device and what it sends over the network.

Questions: github.com/fkupper, or the support email on the Flip Treasure Google Play listing.

What this policy covers

It applies to the Flip Treasure Android app. It does not apply to Google Play itself, your Google account, or other apps on your phone.

What stays on your device

The game saves progress and settings on your phone (the current voyage, Captain’s Log, sound and music switches, and similar local state). That save is needed for the game to work. Flip Treasure does not create an account, and it does not upload your save to Pingul.

If Android backup is enabled on your phone, Google may back up that local data with your Google account. That backup is controlled by Android / Google, not by a Pingul server.

Play statistics

Release builds of Flip Treasure send anonymous play statistics so we can see how the voyage, islands, Port, and tools play. Play does not wait on this. The game works without a network connection; events that cannot be sent are not required for progress.

We send, through GameAnalytics:

  • Voyage start, sinking, and winning, including doubloons held
  • Each island start, plunder, or sinking, including haul and how the island was plundered
  • Busts, Uncharted legs, and doubloons gained or spent (with a coarse reason such as plunder, a trinket, a tool, or a reroll)
  • Port offers shown and items bought, and tools used
  • First-time steps on an install (first dig, Set sail, Port, and opening the Pirate’s Code from the menu)
  • Whether the voyage is Uncharted, and whether a developer tool was used on it
  • That a voyage save or profile could not be read, and similar game errors

We do not send your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or the contents of other apps.

What GameAnalytics adds

Statistics are processed by GameAnalytics, a third-party analytics service. Their SDK also sends technical data so sessions can be counted, including device model, manufacturer, operating system version, app version, connection type (for example Wi‑Fi), timestamps, a session id, and a device or advertising identifier.

GameAnalytics acts as a processor on our behalf. Their own policy is at gameanalytics.com/privacy.

Who we share data with

  • GameAnalytics — play statistics and the technical data above, to run the analytics service
  • Google — as the operator of Google Play (distribution, crash and store services they provide) and, if you use it, Android backup of on-device saves

We do not sell your data. We do not use this information to show you third-party ads in Flip Treasure. This version of the game has no ads and no in-app purchases that go through a Pingul account.

Children

Flip Treasure is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will delete analytics data we can identify.

How long data is kept

On-device saves stay until you clear the app’s data or uninstall. Analytics events are kept by GameAnalytics according to their retention practices and our use of their service (to understand how the game is played over time).

Your choices

This version does not yet include an in-game switch to turn play statistics off. You can stop the app sending further events by uninstalling it. You can also use Android advertising-id / ads personalization settings on your device, which may affect advertising identifiers used by SDKs.

To ask what we hold or to ask us to delete analytics data we can identify, contact us using the details at the top of this page. We will need enough information to find the right records; we do not have your name on file.

Permissions

The app uses network access to send play statistics, and vibration for short haptic feedback. It does not need contacts, camera, microphone, or location permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. Material changes will also be reflected in the Google Play Data safety form where required.
